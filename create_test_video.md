# Quick Ways to Get a Test Video

## Option A: Free Sample Videos
Download a short speech sample from these sources:

1. **Pexels Videos** (Free, no signup)
   - Go to https://www.pexels.com/search/videos/speaking/
   - Filter by duration: < 1 minute
   - Download any person speaking

2. **Pixabay Videos** (Free, no signup)
   - Go to https://pixabay.com/videos/search/presentation/
   - Download any short clip with speech

3. **Sample Speech Video**
   - Go to https://sample-videos.com/
   - Download a sample presentation video

## Option B: Text-to-Speech Video

Use Windows built-in tools:

1. Open PowerPoint
2. Create a slide with text
3. Use "Record Slideshow" feature
4. Enable text-to-speech narration
5. Export as video (File → Export → Create Video)

## Option C: Simple Screen Recording

1. Press `Windows + G` (Game Bar)
2. Click "Record" button
3. Read a paragraph out loud for 30 seconds
4. Stop recording
5. Find video in C:\Users\[YourName]\Videos\Captures\

## Option D: Use Windows Voice Recorder + Combine

1. Open "Voice Recorder" app
2. Record yourself speaking for 30 seconds
3. Use a free tool like Canva to convert audio → video
   - Go to canva.com
   - Create blank video
   - Upload audio
   - Export as MP4
